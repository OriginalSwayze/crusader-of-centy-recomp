## Crusader of Centy Recomp 1.0.1

A fix for widescreen.

- **Broken letters and fences in the fields beside Soleil Town.** With widescreen on, leaving the town left pieces of its map behind in the background layer. In the fields next to it they showed through the water tiles as letter-like columns, stray "K" and "ON" shapes and broken fences. Widescreen now waits until the new area is fully set up before it redraws the sides of the screen.

A save made while the glitch was on screen still contains it. Walk through any door, or into a building and back out, to clear it.

Updating keeps your settings, saves and save states.
